Deployment and recovery rehearsal
--------------------------------

Owner: shared deployment maintainer and SECS deployment owner. Defer only the
deployment-specific rehearsal: no rollout is authorized or performed. Revisit
before deploying a selected installation.

The eventual deployment still needs its actual blocked-drain and native
containment procedures rehearsed under the common design. A finite Compose
stop grace is not proof that an unacknowledged report drains successfully.
An unconfirmed native owner keeps its sources and exclusion until settlement
is proved; status/socket disappearance or a replacement scratch root is not
that proof. Do not build provider-local recovery machinery just to avoid this
qualification. Any shared online barrier must establish its real continuity
preconditions; failed proof remains held for explicit operator resolution.
Disposable fixtures exercise pending-report drain and ordered stop. They do not
prove the original owner's cleanup or continuity on an installed deployment.
Rehearse those deployment-specific procedures before cutover.
