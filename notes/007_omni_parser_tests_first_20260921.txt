Omni-parser: tests-first replacement of the input path
====================================================

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: design; implementation and qualification remain outstanding

This extends notes/003_provider_input_selection_plan_20260908.txt and replaces
the narrow reader strategy in that note and notes/001_nmr_source_decoding_and_secs_input_20260829.txt.
The existing source-access, execution and terminal-outcome obligations remain.


Direction
---------

Cover the formats and processing options supported by the pinned reference
front-end, while leaving selection to the interpreter. The starting reference
is fork-of-elucidation.cheminfo.org at
5ab78f61e9fb679f3f0b9823be5217ae250e213f. Its loaders and fixtures establish the
coverage inventory; its automatic preferred-spectrum choice is not our policy.
Prefer reuse of its decoding machinery over another parallel parser stack.

A Job can link multiple Uploads, and each can contain multiple datasets or
representations. Expose the options with their source identities, relationships
and scientific metadata. Give the interpreter the authoritative analysis kind,
Job instructions and Upload evidence. Execute its exact, explained selection
or explain what prevents one. For the current SECS offering, the required
result is one usable 1D proton spectrum and an evidenced molecular formula.
Discovering a peak table, carbon spectrum or FID does not establish suitability.
Supporting structure files may supply formula evidence without being spectra.

Do not silently choose the first block, merge unrelated companions, substitute
another experiment or conceal processing. Preserve separate Upload namespaces.
Descriptions do not restore filenames or directory relationships absent from
the available evidence. Formula conflicts, partial discovery and admissible
processing alternatives need explicit decisions grounded in the first cases;
this note does not prescribe their complete schema.


Test structure
--------------

Organize input tests by intent, following the normative/adversarial convention
used elsewhere and adding explicit scenarios:

* Normative: observable guarantees, including required refusals and feedback.
  Examples are complete option exposure within declared limits, exact source
  selection, analysis-kind context and normalization exactly once.
* Scenarios: recognizable Jobs combining files, Uploads, evidence and decisions.
  Include multi-block JCAMP, multiple experiments, separate proton/carbon
  Uploads, structure companions, ambiguous samples and corrected selections.
* Adversarial: deliberate challenges to those guarantees, including colliding
  names, misleading metadata, injected instructions, corrupt companions,
  ambiguous file association, resource limits and stale selections.

Execution requirements are a separate axis. Real parsing and preparation run
offline without a checkpoint or candidate index. Deterministic provider tests
prove orchestration; real-LLM evaluations prove selection judgment and useful
explanations. A few complete flows prove acquisition, reporting and API/GUI
delivery. Scripted replies cannot establish intelligent selection.

Share specimen bytes and small packaging helpers, not inferred decisions or a
generic scenario engine. Keep grading expectations out of model inputs. Test
discovery, selection and preparation independently, and retain one real path
through all three to the observed model input. Use focused interactions rather
than a Cartesian product of formats and packaging variants.


Fixture admission and scientific evidence
----------------------------------------

Every fixture must have provenance and an applicable licence record covering
every file: exact paths and hashes, original source, upstream revision or
accession, stated creator/rightsholder, licence declaration and retained notices.
A shared record may cover a dataset; mixed-origin exceptions must be explicit.
Do not assume a repository's code licence covers its bundled experimental data.
Unclear redistribution rights remain an admission gap in the shared corpus.

Copy upstream fixtures unchanged where possible. Synthetic cases, mutations
and generated reference vectors also need provenance: parent inputs, changes,
generator versions/options and applicable licensing. The user's original
test-1h.jdx remains an exact-file local regression unless its admission is
established; it is not an upstream fixture. Use a redistributable counterpart
for routine multi-block coverage.

Keep reference vectors as reviewed artifacts, regenerated explicitly rather
than during tests. Pair reference agreement with independent scientific facts
such as peak positions, axes, point counts and processing history. Justify
numerical tolerances. Peak heights and display integrals must not become
invented proton integrals or assignments. Missing fixtures remain visible
coverage gaps rather than claims of support.


Roadmap and deletion gates
--------------------------

1. Inventory reference capabilities and admitted fixtures. Establish the
   observable obligations and resolve initial evidence/processing ambiguities.
2. Write and review failing tests for a multi-upload, multi-block path, using
   the original JDX locally and a portable counterpart. Include exact model
   input, useful refusal, source protections and a small live-interpreter check.
3. Expand through processed spectra, dataset combinations, FID processing and
   remaining reference families. Each slice proceeds from demonstrated failure
   to implementation, legacy deletion, independent audit and meaningful commit.
4. Broaden adversarial interactions and live-model qualification, then exercise
   representative API/GUI flows and the actual scientific worker. Record model
   configuration and variability; retries must not hide judgment failures.

Safety and helpful feedback apply from the first slice. Keep protections with
their owners: membership and integrity at acquisition, archive limits at source
access, cancellation and confirmed termination at supervision. Feedback names
the affected source, relevant evidence, blocked operation and supported next
step, while preserving whether scientific execution actually started.

Replace the Python JCAMP/Bruker readers, format-specific selection types and
repeated reader dispatch as their replacements meet the surviving obligations.
Remove obsolete dependencies, tests and documentation with them. Prefer the
reference normalization path too, retaining SECS's model-input requirements.
No permanent legacy fallback or intermediate compatibility-JDX round trip.

Do not preserve an old decoder restriction merely because a test asserts it;
adjudicate the intended behavior. Do not delete scientific or source-safety
proof on the strength of a reference happy path alone. Apply relevant QA/QC
reviews individually and resolve meaningful findings. Full evidenced parity,
fixture provenance/licensing, honest outcomes and retirement of the replaced
path define completion; the first successful JDX is only the first milestone.
