Unified scientific Job input
============================

Date: 2026-09-21
Revised: 2026-09-26
Status: target architecture; the pinned reference decoder does not qualify at
this revision and the current Python decoders are transitional

This note supersedes the input-selection design in notes 001 and 003. The
current worker provides per-Upload or exact-member inspection, opaque
representation identities, formula evidence, and Python format-specific
discovery and preparation. The target below adds Job-wide acquisition,
advertised preparation-option identities, a versioned decoder boundary and a
structured support catalog with build-bound qualification evidence.


Objective
---------

A Job may link several Uploads, and each Upload may contain several datasets,
spectra, FIDs, peak tables or structures. Inspect the complete Job input, report
every recognized representation with its exact sources and relationships, and
give those choices to the interpreter together with the provider's analysis
kind. Execute the interpreter's exact choice or report why no choice is
executable.

For SECS, an executable choice requires one 1D proton spectrum and an evidenced
molecular formula. Carbon spectra, peak tables, structures and incomplete data
remain visible because they can explain the choice or failure, but they are not
silently substituted for the required spectrum.

The finished system satisfies these invariants:

* Every linked Upload is acquired and inspected before selection; the
  interpreter cannot choose to skip one. An acquisition, decoder-operation or
  inventory-limit failure names the incomplete scope and prevents selection.
  An attributed unsupported or malformed item may coexist with a usable
  representation when the selected source closure and its evidence are
  complete.
* Decoding enumerates choices; it never selects a preferred spectrum.
* Upload order, descriptions and decoder enumeration order do not establish
  identity or executability. Format-defined member paths may establish dataset
  membership, but a filename alone cannot establish scientific suitability or
  preference.
* A representation identity binds the exact Upload members, byte identities,
  component roles, decoder version and format-local locator needed to execute
  it. Preparation neither widens that source closure nor rediscovers the Job.
* Structured selection fields control execution. Explanation text cannot
  repair an invalid representation, formula source or preparation option.
* Format decoding, FID processing and SECS model preparation each have one
  owner. Resampling and normalization occur exactly once.
* Discovery without an executable selection is an input failure. An executed
  search with no starting candidates is a completed, inconclusive analysis.
* No production fallback retains a second decoder for the same representation.


Authority chain
---------------

    Job Upload membership and acquired bytes
        -> complete bounded Job source manifest
        -> decoder inventory
        -> canonical Job inventory
        -> interpreter selection or input-problem report
        -> verified representation claim
        -> scientific preparation
        -> SECS inference and report

The NMR API owns current Job membership. Acquisition owns read authorization
and byte identity. SourceAccess owns safe direct-file and archive-member access,
namespace separation, integrity and resource limits; it does not recognize
scientific formats.

Acquisition materializes every linked Upload before interpretation. It binds a
sorted membership and content fingerprint to the manifest, then rereads current
Job membership before exposing the inventory. A changed Job discards the
candidate manifest and restarts acquisition; preparation rejects a claim bound
to another manifest. The complete Job must fit the declared count and byte
limits. Exceeding a limit is an attributed Job-input failure rather than an
invitation to inspect a convenient subset.

SourceAccess expands each admitted archive once and produces a complete
logical-object manifest grouped by Upload. The decoder receives that manifest
in one request. There is no companion-file callback that would split traversal
policy between SourceAccess and the decoder.

The decoder owns content recognition, format-local companion roles, dataset
graphs, native axes and intensities, and FID processing. It cannot fetch URLs,
reopen unchecked archives or infer associations between separate Uploads from
matching names.

The input adapter projects decoder results into the canonical Job inventory,
owns opaque Attempt-local execution claims and exposes exact preparation. The
source manifest, decoder dataset graph and Job inventory remain distinct
artifacts: an authority grant, format-specific scientific results, and the
common selection view respectively. The inventory contains representation
kind, sources, scientific metadata, advertised preparation options,
relationships and localized issues. It does not expose reader names or decoder
controls.

The interpreter owns the semantic decision and cites formula evidence from the
admitted Job specification or discovered structures. It selects a
representation identity, preparation-option identity and formula, and explains
the choice. It sees the analysis kind as provider-owned context rather than
inferring it from Job prose. If the evidence is missing, ambiguous or
unsuitable, it reports the blocking facts and the useful next input action
instead of guessing.

Job instructions and Upload descriptions may express user intent, but they are
not decoded scientific metadata. A failure decision includes a blocker kind
and references to the relevant inventory issues, representations or missing
SECS requirements alongside its explanation. The boundary validates those
references; prose alone cannot establish that no executable choice exists.

The interpretation boundary verifies exact Job-specification quotes. The
scientific worker revalidates representation-backed evidence and the executable
selection, admits the choice to the SECS contract, prepares the canonical
Float32 model input and records observed processing facts. The report is
derived from that verified prepared object.

Completeness does not erase failure ownership. Unsupported or malformed input
is an attributed input disposition. A localized issue may leave other complete
representations selectable, but its unknown contents cannot supply a source or
evidence for that selection. Declared input-limit excess is an input failure;
acquisition faults, decoder crashes, timeouts and corrupt decoder protocol are
operational failures. Either failure blocks the incomplete Job inventory from
issuing selectable claims.


Decoder boundary
----------------

Define a repository-owned, versioned decoder protocol at the offline scientific
worker boundary. Evaluate a one-shot process first and choose process placement
only after the spike measures startup, containment and resource needs. Prefer
the pinned low-level libraries used by the reference frontend so that format
comparisons do not depend on a parallel reimplementation. Do not import the
frontend's preferred-spectrum selection, UI messages or Job policy.

Before adopting those libraries, a bounded spike must establish that they can:

* enumerate every representation in the migration floor and every
  reference-target case proposed for the offering rather than return only one
  preferred spectrum;
* return dataset relationships, ordered source closure, stable format-local
  locators, native data and observed processing history without a second local
  format-name or companion inventory;
* cover the current processed-Bruker pair case or permit a narrow extension
  behind the same decoder protocol;
* run without browser or network authority under measured time, memory, output
  and temporary-space limits;
* terminate as part of the worker process tree on timeout, cancellation,
  disconnect, worker failure and container shutdown; and
* be distributed and operated under reviewed licence terms.

If an upstream API requires us to recreate block walking, companion tables,
resource resolution or locator semantics for a format family, that policy is
our decoder implementation rather than reused upstream behavior and must be
named and qualified as such. If a gate fails, choose another licensed decoding
implementation behind the same canonical protocol. Do not ship the spike beside
the Python stack as a fallback. A daemon or sidecar is justified only by
measured startup cost or a required resource and security boundary; it must
preserve the same protocol and ownership.

The decoder package has its own minimal manifest, lockfile, licence inventory
and provenance record. Production images are built from repository-contained,
pinned artifacts without a sibling frontend checkout or runtime network. The
decoder and protocol versions are part of the worker image identity and of each
execution claim.

The pinned reference stack does not qualify as the production decoder at the
evaluated revision. Its low-level JCAMP parser can expose the entry tree needed
to keep linked blocks, complex channels and two-dimensional rows distinct, but
the canonical inventory and positional locators remain project-owned
projections.
The general core returns no representation for the maintained `1r` plus
`procs` Bruker case, and its generic expanded-file path does not preserve the
NMRium state and resource relationship. Its JCAMP and core package manifests
also declare `CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0`; production use has not been approved under
those terms.

Do not fill those gaps with a second companion table, resource resolver or
identity scheme around this stack. None of its packages enters production
unless a later qualification and licence review clears it. Retain the fixtures,
pinned expected outputs and spike observations. Delete the candidate probe and
its tests, or replace them with production-protocol conformance tests cited by
the support catalog, when JCAMP migrates.

The production decoder is one repository-owned protocol and dataset graph,
with one implementation owner per format family. Its components must pass the
qualification suite under reviewed production-compatible licence terms.
Migrate qualified behavior from the current stack one format family at a time,
deleting each displaced discovery and preparation path in the same slice.


Representation and preparation contract
---------------------------------------

The canonical inventory is independent of any decoder's response schema. A
representation contains:

* an opaque identity;
* kind: processed spectrum, FID, peak table or structure;
* exact source references and component roles;
* nucleus, dimensionality and other observed scientific metadata;
* advertised preparation-option identities and their scientific effects;
* related representation identities; and
* completeness or attributed issues.

Inventory coverage and representation usability are separate facts. Coverage
is complete when every object in the Job manifest has an attributed
disposition, including unsupported or malformed. Traversal failure, decoder
failure or bounded-output truncation makes coverage incomplete and blocks all
selection. A representation is usable only when its own required source closure
and preparation option are complete.

A representation identity binds the Attempt and Job-manifest fingerprint,
ordered source closure and byte identities, format-local locator, decoder
version and component roles. A preparation-option identity additionally binds
the processing operation and parameters. Ambiguous archive members are
rejected; ZIP ordinals are not hidden identities.

Selection names one advertised preparation option exactly; there is no global
rule that guesses a processing mode from representation kind. Preparation
resolves only that claim. The decoder owns conversion from the stored format
and FID processing. The SECS preparation boundary owns the final ppm window,
resampling and min-max normalization. The migration must delete any displaced
normalization path and prove the final inference-port vector rather than
compare an intermediate representation.


Sources of truth and qualification
----------------------------------

There is no single format-name allowlist. Different facts have different
owners:

* each format implementation owns what bytes it can recognize and decode;
* the canonical inventory schema owns how discovered choices are represented;
* SECS admission code owns which represented choices are executable;
* the interpreter tool schema owns the required decision and evidence;
* fixture provenance owns byte hashes, origin, licence and derivation;
* direct contract tests own case-specific expected inventory, failure and
  preparation observations;
* a reference lock owns the pinned comparison revision and source hashes;
* a versioned extracted reference-target inventory, when available, owns
  upstream capability keys and applicability decisions;
* a structured support catalog owns stable public claim identities, wording,
  limits and required evidence dimensions; and
* a qualification receipt owns the result that named evidence passed for one
  exact qualification-input digest.

The reference lock owns the current comparison baseline. The reference
frontend is an update and evidence source, not production policy. A claim to
cover all of its formats requires an exhaustive inventory extracted from its
pinned loader or plugin registry. If no such authority can be extracted,
qualify named cases and do not claim full frontend parity.

The support catalog is product authority, not runtime admission policy. Each
claim records only its stable identity, public wording and limits, and exact
pairs of required evidence dimension and evidence identity. It does not copy
fixture provenance, expected representations, tolerances, decoder revisions
or reference revisions from their stronger owners. The provider Hello
`analysis_offerings` and any published support table are projections of this
catalog rather than separately maintained claim lists.

Every capability in the extracted reference target, when such an inventory is
available, has an explicit applicability decision. Comparison with the current
qualification receipt reports each applicable capability as qualified or a
visible gap. Absence is not a classification.

The first catalog freezes a migration floor through reviewed reconciliation of
the current provider offering with passing evidence; overstated wording is
corrected rather than admitted as a claim. The extracted reference target is
tracked separately; its gaps remain visible without silently becoming current
product commitments. A new decoder cannot define completeness by returning a
narrower set. Release receipts preserve what passed for a released build.
Removing a claim is an explicit product change rather than a silent narrowing.

Compatibility is multidimensional: admission, representation enumeration,
metadata agreement, inference-port numerical agreement, real-instrument
qualification and deployed Job flow are separate evidence dimensions. A
shared fixture recognized by two implementations proves only admission unless
the stronger comparison runs.

The qualification command verifies the reference lock and decoder artifact
manifest, runs the direct tests named by active claims, and emits a receipt
containing the passed dimension/evidence pairs and exact build identities. It
also records one digest
over the catalog, evidence tests, runner, fixture provenance and bytes,
reference lock and artifacts, generated offering projections, and decoder
build identity. Publication requires that digest to match its inputs and the
decoder artifact being published, every active claim pair to appear in the
receipt, and the canonical `analysis_offerings` projection and published
support-table bytes to match the catalog. Adding or widening a claim fails
until the missing evidence passes. Runtime does not compare submitted data
with fixture rows and cannot make a broader qualification claim merely because
a variant was admitted.

Reference artifacts are regenerated only through an explicit, pinned, offline
update lane and reviewed as source changes. Ordinary tests never rewrite them.
A derived artifact's provenance cites the reference-lock identity and its own
origin; it does not copy the lock's revision or source hashes as peer
authority. A reference-lock update regenerates only affected oracle artifacts.
A production-decoder update runs every claim against the unchanged oracle and
cannot rewrite its own evidence.


Tests and fixtures
------------------

Keep the normative, scenario and adversarial suites organized by observable
contract:

* Normative tests prove complete enumeration within each declared fixture and
  bounded Job scope, stable source identities, exact selection, formula
  evidence, processing facts and final model input.
* Scenario tests exercise realistic Jobs with multiple Uploads, experiments,
  nuclei, structures, annotations and partial failures.
* Adversarial tests challenge namespace separation, companion association,
  stale claims, misleading metadata, corrupt data, duplicate names, resource
  limits, decoder failures and cancellation.

Keep format behavior in direct tests rather than transcribing it into the
support catalog or a generic scenario language. Use data tables only where
variants share identical mechanics. Scripted interpreter replies prove
transport and orchestration; real-model evaluations prove selection judgment
and useful failure explanations. A small number of deployed API/GUI flows
prove the full handoff.

Every committed fixture and derived artifact requires recorded provenance,
licence, exact hash and derivation. Synthetic data records its generator and
scientific construction. Upstream data retains notices and revision. A local
customer or user file remains a private regression probe until redistribution
rights establish that it can enter the shared corpus.

The pinned reference lock records several decoding dependencies under
CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0, and the pinned frontend tree has no root licence file. Their
use in fixture generation does not establish permission to redistribute or run
them in production. Production adoption remains blocked until authoritative
terms, attribution and the intended service use have been reviewed.


Migration and deletion
----------------------

Migration slices change one authority at a time and end without dual ownership:

1. Consolidate the reference lock, structure the provider support catalog and
   bind its migration-floor claims to existing direct-test evidence. Generate
   offering projections and a build qualification receipt without changing
   runtime admission policy. Correct overstated documentation.
2. Decide the localized-issue selection rule with red acceptance cases, then
   replace interpreter-directed per-source inspection with owned Job-wide
   acquisition and inventory. Prove membership fencing, classification of every
   linked Upload, and refusal when count, byte or inventory-size limits prevent
   complete Job inspection.
3. The pinned reference stack did not qualify for production use at the
   evaluated revision. Retain its fixtures, expected outputs and observations;
   do not retain its project-owned inventory projection as a second parser.
4. Establish the production protocol, reproducible dependency build and
   process-tree supervision around qualified decoder components under reviewed
   production-compatible licence terms. This slice claims no new format support.
5. Move the complete JCAMP-DX family through the decoder boundary, including
   processed spectra, multiple blocks, peak tables, unsuitable dimensions and
   FIDs. Introduce advertised preparation-option identities in that slice and
   delete JCAMP's displaced Python discovery, preparation, dispatch,
   dependencies and implementation-shaped tests.
6. Move each remaining family in a separate ownership slice: structures and
   annotations, processed Bruker, JEOL, NMRium resources, Bruker FIDs and
   Varian FIDs. Each slice preserves the canonical contract, adds its
   qualification evidence and leaves one production owner.
7. Qualify multiple-Upload selection with the live interpreter and deployed
   API/GUI flow. Remove remaining format dispatch and transitional
   documentation. Verify by
   repository-wide absence checks that no second decoder or normalization path
   remains.

Each slice begins with a failing acceptance or evolution proof for its stated
invariant. A format migration is mergeable only when that format has one
production discovery and preparation owner; comparison scaffolding does not
ship.

The refactor is complete only when all linked Uploads participate in one
inventory, every entry in the migration floor is qualified on the
replacement path or removed by a separate offering decision, and every
representation returned by the adopted decoder is projected through the
canonical contract or rejected with an attributed reason. SECS admission
remains explicit, the interpreter selects an exact executable choice or reports
the evidenced blocker, every published compatibility claim is backed by a
qualification receipt for the released build, and the superseded Python format
stack is absent. A gap in the migration floor or an advertised category does
not satisfy completion; other reference-target gaps remain explicit
qualification work.
