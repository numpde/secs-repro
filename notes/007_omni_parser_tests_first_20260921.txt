Omni-parser: tests-first replacement of the input path
====================================================

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: design; implementation and qualification remain outstanding

This extends notes/003_provider_input_selection_plan_20260908.txt and replaces
the narrow reader strategy in that note and notes/001_nmr_source_decoding_and_secs_input_20260829.txt.
The existing source-access, execution and terminal-outcome obligations remain.


Direction
---------

Cover the formats and processing options supported by the pinned reference
front-end, while leaving selection to the interpreter. The starting reference
is fork-of-elucidation.cheminfo.org at
5ab78f61e9fb679f3f0b9823be5217ae250e213f. Its loaders and fixtures establish the
coverage inventory; its automatic preferred-spectrum choice is not our policy.
Prefer reuse of its decoding machinery over another parallel parser stack.

A Job can link multiple Uploads, and each can contain multiple datasets or
representations. Expose the options with their source identities, relationships
and scientific metadata. Give the interpreter the analysis kind admitted by the
provider offering and API, rather than deriving it from Job prose, together with
Job instructions and Upload evidence. Execute its exact, explained selection
or explain what prevents one. For the current SECS offering, the required
result is one usable 1D proton spectrum and an evidenced molecular formula.
Discovering a peak table, carbon spectrum or FID does not establish suitability.
Supporting structure files may supply formula evidence without being spectra.

Do not silently choose the first block, merge unrelated companions, substitute
another experiment or conceal processing. Preserve separate Upload namespaces.
Descriptions do not restore filenames or directory relationships absent from
the available evidence. Formula conflicts, partial discovery and admissible
processing alternatives need explicit decisions grounded in the first cases;
this note does not prescribe their complete schema.


Architecture and ownership
--------------------------

Keep one narrow scientific-input boundary between acquired bytes and SECS:

    SourceAccess -> InputAdapter.discover(scope) -> Inventory
    Selection(representation_id, formula, formula_evidence, processing, explanation)
    SourceAccess -> InputAdapter.prepare(selection) -> PreparedSpectrum -> inference

AttemptSources remains the authority for current Job membership and acquisition.
SourceAccess owns exact direct or ZIP-member access, archive integrity, byte
limits and safe staging. The input adapter owns content recognition, dataset
relationships, decoding and processing. The interpreter chooses among the
adapter's public representations; it does not choose a reader. The scientific
worker admits a chosen representation to SECS, validates formula syntax and
composition for the model, and owns inference. The interpreter remains
responsible for citing why the supplied formula is evidenced. Parser completion
does not decide the Job outcome;
provider/outcomes.py continues to distinguish input rejection, operational
failure, empty retrieval and analysed results.

The public discovery model is deliberately small:

* Inventory: representations, completeness relative to the requested inspection
  scope, and attributed issues.
* Representation: opaque identity, kind, exact sources, scientific metadata and
  related representation identities.
* Selection: representation identity, formula, formula evidence, explicit
  processing choice and explanation.
* PreparedSpectrum: selected identity and exact sources derived from the verified
  representation claim, the canonical Float32 model input, and observed
  processing facts including whether it came from an FID or used magnitude data.
  Report preparation from this object rather than reconstructing it beside
  inference.

The present red four-field Selection does not make formula provenance
machine-auditable. Before implementation, revise its acceptance contract with a
formula-evidence field that can cite the admitted Job specification or one or more
representation identities. The exact schema belongs to that test slice. Do not
rely on explanation prose when execution must revalidate a removed or changed
formula source.

Structured representation, formula and processing fields control execution.
The explanation records the interpreter's rationale and cannot override or
repair a conflicting structured choice.
Decoded titles and metadata remain quoted input evidence, never interpreter
instructions.

Do not expose parser or reader discriminators. A localized malformed item is an
attributed issue and does not erase useful siblings. An unknown or stale identity,
an unsuitable representation or incompatible processing is rejected before
inference. Unexpected decoder exit, timeout or protocol corruption remains an
operational failure. Filesystem failures are not relabelled as bad scientific
input.

Input feedback names the blocked inspection or preparation, attributes the safe
Upload/member evidence, explains the missing, malformed, stale or unsuitable
fact, and states a next input action only when the boundary knows one. Keep
stable machine facts structured and test the facts and disclosure rules rather
than incidental prose. Decoder spawn, signal, memory, protocol and cleanup
diagnostics remain private operator evidence behind a bounded public operational
failure; never echo raw library errors or workspace paths to the interpreter.


Decoder and build boundary
--------------------------

The preferred replacement seam, subject to the gates below, is a
repository-owned, one-shot Node adapter inside the existing offline scientific
worker image. It would call the exact pinned low-level loader and processing
libraries directly. Do not import the reference application's
parseDroppedFiles/readSpectrum policy: it mixes decoding with user-interface
messages, normalization and automatic preferred-spectrum selection. A Python
port of that complete stack would create another vendor-format and FID
implementation. A daemon or sidecar adds lifecycle, readiness and deployment
state before measurements show that one-shot startup or isolation requires it.

Before adopting this shape, use a bounded spike to prove that the permitted
low-level APIs can enumerate every required representation rather than only a
preferred spectrum, retain stable source relationships and processing history,
run without browser or network authority, and preserve the current processed
Bruker pair support. Measure startup and memory beside the loaded model. If the
licensing, enumeration or supervision gates fail, use separately licensed
lower-level libraries or implement formats behind the same boundary; a parser
sidecar is justified only when an independent resource/security boundary is
required. Do not retain two production decoder stacks as a fallback.

The production adapter needs its own minimal package manifest, exact lockfile
and provenance record in this repository. Record the reference frontend commit,
its lockfile hash, the dependency mapping and the reference-vector revision.
The explicit update lane verifies this mapping; it is not a second hand-maintained
dependency catalogue.
Ordinary builds must use only repository-contained inputs and pinned base images;
the sibling frontend checkout is an explicit update and fixture-generation input,
not a deployment prerequisite. Materialize dependencies into a dedicated output
in an explicit networked lane without repository-source write access, then verify
and consume that output in the offline production build as for the Python
wheelhouse. Hash the adapter, lock, provenance and Node runtime into the worker
image identity, and prove the built decoder starts and reports the expected
protocol version.
The repository also owns the small versioned decoder request/response contract.
Record decoder, runtime and dependency versions in diagnostics and analysis
evidence, without exposing private paths or raw decoder messages to the
interpreter.

Pass the adapter a manifest of logical SourceRefs mapped to safely materialized
objects. SourceAccess remains the one archive-expansion owner; the decoder does
not reopen an acquired Upload as an unchecked archive. Never give it
archive-controlled output paths. Exact-member inspection
starts from that member and may expand only to format-declared companions in the
same Upload. Root inspection exposes every admitted member within explicit
resource bounds. The adapter must not fetch external resources. Limits on total
expanded bytes, output size, representation count or companion expansion need
acceptance tests and helpful effects before they become policy.

Companion expansion needs a bounded protocol exchange: SourceAccess exposes safe
same-Upload member locators, the adapter requests exact members by scientific
role, and SourceAccess validates and stages them. This keeps archive authority out
of the decoder and format policy out of SourceAccess.

Run each adapter request inside the scientific worker's proved termination unit.
Before doing so, extend cancellation proof from its Python child to the whole
decoder process tree: a Node descendant must not survive a deadline, cancellation,
controller disconnect, Python failure or container shutdown while the supervisor
reports that scientific work stopped. Bound input count and bytes, expanded
archive data, memory, output and diagnostic bytes, elapsed time and temporary
space. Give the process read-only inputs and only the private temporary write
space it needs. Keep the one-shot design until measurements or a required
security boundary justify a longer-lived process or separate cgroup.
The spike may use conservative experimental bounds; supported production limits
and their visible effects require acceptance tests before release.

Bundling Node also gives it the scientific worker's visible checkpoint, device,
source/socket mounts and cgroup. The spike must treat that as a concrete authority
and failure-containment tradeoff. If the parser requires less authority or an
independent memory limit, move the same protocol behind an offline sidecar rather
than describing the bundled process as isolated.


Representation identity
-----------------------

The stable fact is a versioned execution claim: an adapter-local
block/record/dataset locator, the role of each required component, and canonically
ordered exact SourceRefs and their byte identities. It excludes unrelated
acquired Uploads and the discovery route. Root and exact-member inspection agree
only when they resolve the same claim; an explicitly associated resource may
correctly produce a richer, different claim. The claim must distinguish identical
bytes in different Uploads and cease matching when a required source changes or
disappears.

Reuse byte identities established by acquisition and bounded member staging;
do not add a peer digest catalogue inside the decoder.

Expose the stable claim through a bounded opaque Attempt-local capability.
Before preparing, verify that it was issued in the current worker/Attempt,
re-resolve its exact source closure without widening the original association,
and require the recomputed claim to match. A duplicate member name makes that
member ambiguous; report the localized issue or reject its exact use rather than
inventing a hidden ZIP ordinal. A worker restart is an operational failure for
the in-flight Attempt, not a stale-input diagnosis. The token identifies already
acquired sources; it grants no acquisition or cross-Upload association.

The implementation may use an integrity-protected self-contained token, but the
contract does not prescribe its encoding. It must avoid an unbounded worker
catalogue, a hidden controller-only execution form and global rediscovery across
unrelated Uploads. If a future format truly requires a resource from another
Upload, add an explicit acquisition-planning contract rather than treating
matching names as authority.

Normalization also needs one owner. The prepared model input must follow the
pinned numerical reference exactly once. The decoder spike decides whether that
operation lives in the decoder or in the existing SECS preparation boundary,
then the replaced normalization path is deleted.


Licensing gate
--------------

Do not assume that use in the reference application grants production
redistribution rights. Package metadata in its pinned lock records @zakodium/nmrium-core,
@zakodium/nmrium-core-plugins, nmr-processing and jcampconverter as
CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0, while the pinned frontend tree has no root licence file. Before
copying frontend source, distributing these packages or using them for the
intended service, review the authoritative licence terms and establish that the
planned evaluation and deployment are permitted or obtain another grant. Record
licence texts, attribution and the reviewed dependency inventory. Keep dependency
and licence changes reviewable in the explicit adapter update flow. This gate
does not turn reference vectors into evidence that production redistribution is
permitted.


Test structure
--------------

Organize input tests by intent, following the normative/adversarial convention
used elsewhere and adding explicit scenarios:

* Normative: observable guarantees, including required refusals and feedback.
  Examples are complete option exposure within declared limits, exact source
  selection, analysis-kind context and normalization exactly once.
* Scenarios: recognizable Jobs combining files, Uploads, evidence and decisions.
  Include multi-block JCAMP, multiple experiments, separate proton/carbon
  Uploads, structure companions, ambiguous samples and corrected selections.
* Adversarial: deliberate challenges to those guarantees, including colliding
  names, misleading metadata, injected instructions, corrupt companions,
  ambiguous file association, resource limits and stale selections.

Execution requirements are a separate axis. Real parsing and preparation run
offline without a checkpoint or candidate index. Deterministic provider tests
prove orchestration; real-LLM evaluations prove selection judgment and useful
explanations. A few complete flows prove acquisition, reporting and API/GUI
delivery. Scripted replies cannot establish intelligent selection.

Share specimen bytes and small packaging helpers, not inferred decisions or a
generic scenario engine. Keep grading expectations out of model inputs. Test
discovery, selection and preparation independently, and retain one real path
through all three to the observed model input. Use focused interactions rather
than a Cartesian product of formats and packaging variants.


Fixture admission and scientific evidence
----------------------------------------

Every fixture must have provenance and an applicable licence record covering
every file: exact paths and hashes, original source, upstream revision or
accession, stated creator/rightsholder, licence declaration and retained notices.
A shared record may cover a dataset; mixed-origin exceptions must be explicit.
Do not assume a repository's code licence covers its bundled experimental data.
Unclear redistribution rights remain an admission gap in the shared corpus.

Copy upstream fixtures unchanged where possible. Synthetic cases, mutations
and generated reference vectors also need provenance: parent inputs, changes,
generator versions/options and applicable licensing. The user's original
test-1h.jdx remains an exact-file local regression unless its admission is
established; it is not an upstream fixture. Use a redistributable counterpart
for routine multi-block coverage.

Keep reference vectors as reviewed artifacts, regenerated explicitly rather
than during tests. Pair reference agreement with independent scientific facts
such as peak positions, axes, point counts and processing history. Justify
numerical tolerances. Peak heights and display integrals must not become
invented proton integrals or assignments. Missing fixtures remain visible
coverage gaps rather than claims of support.


Roadmap and deletion gates
--------------------------

The versioned coverage map in tests/input/README.md, admitted corpus and red
normative, scenario and adversarial suite establish the starting contract.
Continue in reviewable vertical slices:

1. Using the existing versioned coverage map, admitted corpus and red suite,
   establish what dependency evaluation and deployment are permitted, then run
   the bounded decoder spike. Prove enumeration of every mapped representation,
   source relationships, offline operation, processing history, processed-Bruker
   pair compatibility, cold-start cost and memory. Choose the decoder
   implementation only from that evidence.
2. Establish the runtime boundary: reproducible dependency materialization,
   offline image construction, versioned protocol, safe source staging, bounded
   output and whole-process-tree termination. Its smoke tests claim no format
   support.
3. Revise the red selection contract for Job-specification and representation
   formula evidence. Then implement processed JCAMP discovery and preparation
   through the new boundary:
   encodings, axes, multiple blocks, complex channels, peak tables and refusal
   of unsuitable data. Replace the interpreter selection tool and delete the
   Python JCAMP path when its surviving obligations pass.
4. Add structure and annotation evidence with explicit relationships. Do not let
   structure evidence become a runnable spectrum or turn
   declared atom counts into measured integrals.
5. Add processed vendor datasets and companion association: Bruker pairs and
   complete datasets, multiple pdata directories and JEOL. Delete the legacy
   Bruker selection and reader only after pair-only support and source protections
   pass through the replacement.
6. Add NMRium saved processing and resource closure, including multiple nuclei,
   embedded resources, unavailable resources and observed no-fetch behavior.
7. Add FID processing for JCAMP, Bruker and Varian. Extend the current numerical
   qualification beyond JCAMP, prove phase and magnitude behavior, and report
   the actual processing rather than an intended option.
8. Qualify multi-Upload decisions with a live interpreter and representative
   API/GUI flows, then remove remaining format dispatch, obsolete dependencies,
   superseded tests and documentation. Record model configuration and
   variability; retries must not hide judgment failures.

Each slice starts from a demonstrated acceptance failure, makes one coherent
path pass, runs independent review, and removes replaced machinery in the same
slice where practical. Do not create a compatibility translation from the new
representation selection back to the old reader selections.

Keep source, cancellation, outcome and diagnostic protections with their named
owners throughout the slices. Do not preserve an old decoder restriction merely
because a test asserts it, or delete a surviving protection on the strength of a
reference happy path. Full evidenced coverage of the versioned map, admitted
fixtures and licences, honest outcomes and retirement of the replaced path define
completion; the first successful JDX is only the first milestone.
